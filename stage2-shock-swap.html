    What is real and what is a guess

    • Real: every part number, the two shock screw sizes and thread (M3, 0.5 mm pitch, 17 and 5 turns), the 95 mm shock length, and the shop names, prices and stock, which were read from the live shop pages on 15 October 2026.
    • Guess: the outline of the tower, lower arm, upright, bumper and gearbox housing. They are read from the manual drawing and photos, not measured. Anything marked Stand-in shape on a part card is a guess. The FTX6356 aluminium shock is drawn as the stock shock in silver, because it was not photographed.
    • Not shown: the inside of the shock, the oil, the electronics and the rest of the truck. Prices and stock change, so check the shop before you order.
