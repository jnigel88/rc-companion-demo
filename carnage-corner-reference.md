# FTX Carnage 2.0 Brushless

15 October 2026

One rear corner, for modeling. Taken from the FTX Carnage 2.0 Brushless manual (PDF dated 18 July 2023). The exploded views are printed pages 22–24. The spare-parts photos and names are printed pages 25–31. Page 11 only says that dirty running wears the shocks out, and that oil and seals should be replaced to keep the damping smooth. It gives no oil weight and no build order.

The rear of the truck is the lower part of the suspension drawing on page 24. The shock drawing is the right-hand column on page 23, headed rear shocks. Counts below are what the spare list prints for the whole truck. It does not say how many of each screw go in this single corner.

## Shock

The complete rear shock is **FTX6203**, listed as rear shocks, 2 pcs. One of that pair is this corner. The spare photo shows it assembled: a straight silver body, a coil spring over the body, a thin shaft out the bottom, and an eye at each end. The top eye goes to the shock tower. The bottom eye goes down to the suspension.

Pieces, with the names printed in the list:

- **Body, FTX6209**, rear shock body, 2 pcs. A straight metal cylinder, open at both ends, with a screw thread at the ends. The front body is a different number (FTX6208).
- **Spring, FTX6205**, rear shock spring, 2 pcs. A black coil, wound tighter toward one end, so it is slightly tapered. It slides over the body. The front spring is FTX6204.
- **Shaft, FTX6207**, rear shock shaft and piston set, 2 sets. A thin metal rod. One end is plain. The other end has a small flat piston. On the drawing the rod runs through the body, piston inside, plain end sticking out the bottom. The front shaft is FTX6206. The list says “2 sets” and does not say how many loose pieces are in a set.
- **Upper cap, FTX6210**, shock upper cap, 2 sets. A black cap with a thread on the outside and a hole through the middle. It closes the top of the body. The same number is printed on the front shock column as well.
- **Lower cap, FTX6211**, shock lower cap, 2 sets. A smaller black ring, also threaded, with a hole the shaft passes through. Same shared number on the front column.
- **Lower holder and adjust ring, FTX6212**, 2 sets. The photo shows two black parts together: a knurled ring (the collar that screws along the body and sets the spring) and a flanged piece the spring sits against. Called out on both shock columns.
- **Upper seal, FTX6512**, shock O-ring upper, 6 pcs. **Lower seal, FTX6510**, shock O-ring lower, 6 pcs. Small rings drawn at the two caps.
- **E-clip, FTX6517**, 2.5 mm, 6 pcs. Printed beside the rear shaft.
- **Bottom joint.** **FTX6500**, shock ball end, 6 pcs, and **FTX6506**, ball A, 6 pcs, are both printed at the lower end of the rear shock.

Down the rear shock column the drawing also prints **FASTM3** (the spare list calls this a nylock nut, M3, 6 pcs) and a screw marked **FAST60-40**. That second code is not on the spare-parts pages, so the size is unknown.

The top of this shock bolts to one ear of the rear tower. The tower is one piece for both rear corners: **FTX6201**, rear shock tower, 1 pc. It is a flat black plate with a wide base and two upright ears, and each ear has a hole. This corner uses one ear.

## Lower suspension arm

**FTX6321**, rear lower suspension arms, 2 pcs. The spare photo is a black plastic wishbone. The chassis end is the wide end and has two round holes side by side. The outer end comes to one round hole. Ribs run along the two legs. The list is a pair, so this corner is one arm. The photo does not say whether the two arms are mirror images.

That number is clear on the spare-parts page. It could not be read on the exploded drawing. On that drawing the front lower arm, **FTX6320**, is up at the front of the car. Do not use that number for this corner.

The other arm in the same rear cluster is the upper one, **FTX6328**, rear upper arm, 2 sets. It is thinner than the lower wishbone. The outer hole of the lower arm meets the hub carrier, **FTX6217** (hub carrier uprights, 2 pcs, called out at this rear end).

## Hinge pin

**FTX6223**, hinge pins (long and short), 1 set. The spare photo is a small bag of straight silver rods in two lengths. The list does not say how many pins are in the set, which length belongs to the rear lower arm, or which length belongs to the upper arm.

The number FTX6223 could not be read on the exploded pages. What the rear of page 24 does show is **FTX6221**, rear suspension holders, 2 pcs. Those are the blocks on the chassis that the inner end of the arm hinges in. A separate short pin, **FTX6513**, pin 2×10 mm, 6 pcs, is also printed in that rear cluster. That is a short pin, not the long-and-short hinge-pin set.

Near the outer end of this corner the drawing prints a bearing as **FTBB16 (10×5×4)**.

## Rear bumper

The manual does not name a rear bumper. The only bumper in the spare list is **FTX6324N**, front bumper and brace. It is drawn at the front of the car on page 24, and the number is printed twice there. The spare photo is a wide black plastic bar with a flatter face and a brace behind it. A second listing, **FTX6346**, is front bumper LED lights.

At the rear, the plastic structure on the chassis is the shock tower **FTX6201** and the plate under it, not a part labeled as a bumper.

## Chassis

**FTX6331**, chassis plate, 1 pc. One flat black plastic plate for the whole car. The spare photo shows a low tub: an open middle, raised ribs, and a row of screw holes around the rim. The rear suspension holders and the shock tower sit on the back of this plate. The plate itself is called out on the chassis drawing, printed page 22. The rear arm, holders, tower, and shock are called out on page 24.

## Screws in this corner

Bag quantities are from the spare list. The drawing does not print how many of each go in one corner. Page 11 says to use thread lock on replacement screws that go into metal threads.

Printed on the shock:

- **FASTM3**, nylock nut, M3, bag of 6.
- **FAST60-40**, a screw on both the front and rear shock drawings. Size not listed.

Printed beside the rear tower, holders, and shock on page 24:

- **FTX6526**, button head hex, M3×10, bag of 6. Two callouts next to the rear tower.
- **FTX6536**, flat head hex, M3×10, bag of 6. One callout by the rear holders.
- **FTX6537**, flat head hex, M3×12, bag of 6. More than one callout, and one of them is also marked (M3×12) on the drawing.
- **FTX6538**, flat head hex, M3×14, bag of 6. Several callouts on this rear area.
- **FTX6541**, flat head hex, M3×36, bag of 6. One callout, the long flat head.
- **FAST118**, cap head hex, M3×18, bag of 6. One callout next to the rear shock number.

On the chassis page, **FTX6535**, flat head hex, M3×8, bag of 6, is printed in many places on the plate. Those callouts are not tied to a counted pattern for this corner.

## What the manual does not show

No rear bumper name or drawing. No readable FTX6321 or FTX6223 on the exploded pages. No count of screws, pins, or caps for a single corner, and no statement of how many pieces are inside a “set.” No shock oil weight, fill order, or bleed steps. No lengths, hole diameters, or thread pitch, apart from the screw sizes and the 10×5×4 bearing printed above. No note of whether the two lower arms are left and right mirrors.
