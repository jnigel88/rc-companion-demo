What it does

Holds the top eye of the shock to the tower. A thread this fine moves half a millimetre for every full turn.

What is real and what is a guess

  • Real: the shock’s length, its parts and their names come from the manual spare list and the shop photos. The screw is an actual M3 thread, 0.5 mm pitch.
  • Guess: the little tower stub the screw threads into, the exact blue, and how deep the thread goes. The next steps replace these with checked shapes.
  • Not shown: the inside of the shock. It is solid here.
