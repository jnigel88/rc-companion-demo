What the Carnage manual says

  • Screws: page 11 says to use thread lock on replacement screws that go into metal threads.
  • Shocks: page 11 says dirty running wears them out, and that oil and seals should be replaced to keep damping smooth. It gives no oil weight and no build order.
  • Counts: the spare list gives screw sizes in bags of six. The drawings do not say how many go in one corner.
